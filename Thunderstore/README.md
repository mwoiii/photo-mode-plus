# PhotoModePlus

PhotoModePlus is a continuation of the PhotoMode mods, originally made by CWMlolzlz. In addition to fixing numerous issues (see changelog), this mod aims to eventually expand on the number of features.

# Controls

The basics of camera movement and rotation use your configured keybinds.

- Shift - Increases the camera speed while held.
- L.Ctrl - Reduces the camera speed while held.
- RMB - Vertical mouse movements adjust the field of view while held.
- MMB - Horizontal mouse movements roll the camera while held.
